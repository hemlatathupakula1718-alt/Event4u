Role-Based Governance

How Event4u Connects Campus Stakeholders

Structured permissions and 3-tier approval hierarchy ensuring institutional safety and financial accountability.

1. Principal & Director

High-level institutional oversight, final proposal budget sanctioning, audit reports, and college-wide analytics.

2. Head of Dept (HoD)

Review departmental proposals, verify feasibility & safety, endorse submissions before forwarding to Principal.

3. Faculty Coordinator

Draft proposals, appoint student sub-coordinators, allocate sub-event budgets, and verify student payments.

4. Sub-Coordinator

Manage volunteers, execute sub-events, verify entry passes at gates using the live QR scanner tool.
